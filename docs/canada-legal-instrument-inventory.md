## Detailed Enactment Dossiers

### 1. Criminal Code (ss. 342.1 & 430(1.1) - Computer Offences)
- **Official Citation:** R.S.C., 1985, c. C-46 (ss. 342.1, 342.2, 430(1.1))
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `CODE_PROVISION` | **Year:** 1985
- **Issuing Authority:** Department of Justice Canada / Public Prosecution Service of Canada (PPSC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-46/section-342.1.html`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 342.1:** Unauthorized use of a computer, unauthorized interception of a computer service, and unauthorized possession/use of passwords. Punishable by imprisonment up to **10 years**.
  - **s. 342.2:** Making, possessing, or distributing software or devices for unauthorized computer access (exploit kits, credential harvesters). Imprisonment up to **2 years**.
  - **s. 430(1.1):** Mischief in relation to computer data (destroying, altering, rendering meaningless, or obstructing lawful use). Indictable offence punishable by imprisonment up to **10 years**.

### 2. Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
- **Official Citation:** S.C. 2000, c. 5
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2000
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Division 1.1:** Mandatory reporting of security safeguard breaches: Organizations must notify the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals of any breach creating a **real risk of significant harm (RROSH)** as soon as feasible.
  - **Section 28:** Fines up to **CAD 100,000** per violation for knowingly failing to report breaches or failing to maintain breach logs.

### 3. Bill C-26: Critical Cyber Systems Protection Act (CCSPA)
- **Official Citation:** S.C. 2024 (Bill C-26, Part 2)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2024
- **Issuing Authority:** Communications Security Establishment (CSE / CCCS) / Minister of Public Safety
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.parl.ca/legisinfo/en/bill/44-1/c-26`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 2:** Obligates designated operators in federally regulated critical sectors (telecom, finance, energy, transport) to establish cybersecurity programs, monitor third-party cyber risks, and implement cyber protection directions.
  - **Section 17:** Mandatory **immediate cyber incident reporting** to the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS).
  - **Section 38:** Administrative monetary penalties up to **CAD 15,000,000** for serious contraventions.

### 4. Telecommunications Act (Cyber Security Orders & Safeguards)
- **Official Citation:** S.C. 1993, c. 38 (as amended by Bill C-26)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1993
- **Issuing Authority:** CRTC / Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/t-3.4/`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 15.1:** Authorizes the Minister of Industry to issue binding cyber security orders to telecommunications service providers, including bans on untrusted high-risk foreign equipment vendors.
  - Penalties up to **CAD 10,000,000** per day for continuing non-compliance.

### 5. PIPEDA (Part 2 - Electronic Documents)
- **Official Citation:** S.C. 2000, c. 5, Part 2
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2000
- **Issuing Authority:** Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat / Department of Justice
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-8.html#h-417180`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **ss. 31–48:** Provides for legal recognition of electronic signatures and documents in federal law, establishing legal equivalence between electronic records and traditional paper records.

### 6. OSFI Guideline B-13: Technology and Cyber Risk Management
- **Official Citation:** OSFI Guideline B-13
- **Scope:** `SECTORAL` | **Typology:** `REGULATION` | **Year:** 2022
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/guideline-b-13-technology-cyber-risk-management`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Mandatory technology and cyber risk management controls for federally regulated financial institutions (banks, insurers, trust companies).
  - Mandatory notification to OSFI within **24 hours** of discovering any technology or cyber security incident meeting OSFI's materiality criteria.

### 7. Canada Evidence Act (ss. 31.1–31.8 - Electronic Documents)
- **Official Citation:** R.S.C., 1985, c. C-5 (ss. 31.1–31.8)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1985 (Enacted 2000)
- **Issuing Authority:** Courts of Canada / Department of Justice
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-5/section-31.1.html`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 31.1:** Authentication of electronic documents by evidence capable of supporting a finding that the document is what it purports to be.
  - **s. 31.2 & 31.3:** Satisfies best evidence rule through proof of the integrity of the electronic documents system that produced the record.

### 8. Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL)
- **Official Citation:** S.C. 2010, c. 23
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2010
- **Issuing Authority:** CRTC / Competition Bureau / Privacy Commissioner
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/e-1.6/`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 6:** Prohibits sending commercial electronic messages without express or implied consent and a clear unsubscribe mechanism.
  - **s. 8:** Prohibits installing software or computer programs (spyware, malware, botnets) on another person's device without express consent.
  - Administrative monetary penalties up to **CAD 10,000,000** for corporate offenders.

### 9. Excise Tax Act (GST/HST Digital Economy Measures)
- **Official Citation:** R.S.C., 1985, c. E-15 (Part IX, Division V.1)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1985 (Amended 2021)
- **Issuing Authority:** Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/e-15/`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Division V.1:** Mandates simplified GST/HST registration and collection for non-resident vendors providing digital products, apps, and streaming services to Canadian consumers.
  - Marketplace platform operators are deemed the supplier responsible for tax collection on sales facilitated through their digital platforms.
