## Detailed Enactment Dossiers

### 1. Computer Misuse Act 1993 (CMA)
- **Official Citation:** Cap. 50A (Act 19 of 1993, 2020 Rev Ed, as amended)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1993
- **Issuing Authority:** Singapore Police Force (SPF) / Attorney-General's Chambers (AGC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CMA1993`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 3:** Unauthorized access to computer material. Fine up to **SGD 5,000** or imprisonment up to **2 years** (SGD 10,000 / 3 years for repeat offenders).
  - **s. 5:** Unauthorized modification of computer material. Fine up to **SGD 10,000** or up to **3 years**; up to **20 years** if critical systems or public health are affected.
  - **s. 6:** Unauthorized obstruction of use of computer (DDoS attacks). Fine up to **SGD 10,000** or up to **3 years**.
  - **s. 8A:** Obtaining, retaining, or supplying personal information obtained through an offence. Fine up to **SGD 10,000** or up to **3 years**.

### 2. Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA)
- **Official Citation:** Act 26 of 2012 (2020 Rev Ed, as amended by PDPA Amendment Act 2020)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2012
- **Issuing Authority:** Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 6A:** Mandatory Data Breach Notification: Organizations must assess suspected data breaches and notify the PDPC and affected individuals as soon as practicable, and no later than **3 calendar days (72 hours)** of determining a notifiable breach.
  - **Section 48J:** Financial penalties up to **SGD 1,000,000** or **10% of annual turnover in Singapore** for organizations with annual turnover exceeding SGD 10,000,000.

### 3. Cybersecurity Act 2018 (Amended 2024)
- **Official Citation:** Act 9 of 2018 (as amended by Cybersecurity (Amendment) Act 2024)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2018
- **Issuing Authority:** Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (CSA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CA2018`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 3:** Regulation of Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) across 11 designated essential service sectors.
  - **Section 14:** Mandatory incident reporting: Initial notification to CSA within **2 hours** of confirmation of a prescribed cybersecurity incident.
  - **2024 Amendment:** Expands regulatory oversight to Foundation Digital Infrastructure (FDI - cloud providers and data centers) and Systems of Temporary Cybersecurity Concern (STCC).

### 4. Telecommunications Act 1999 (Cyber Security Provisions)
- **Official Citation:** Cap. 323 (Act 43 of 1999, 2020 Rev Ed)
- **Scope:** `SECTORAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1999
- **Issuing Authority:** Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/TA1999`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Section 26:** Grants IMDA powers to issue binding directions to telecommunication licensees for national security, public safety, and telecommunications cyber defense.
  - Mandatory compliance with the Telecom Cyber Security Code of Practice (TCS-CoP).

### 5. Electronic Transactions Act 2010 (ETA)
- **Official Citation:** Cap. 88 (Act 16 of 2010, 2020 Rev Ed, as amended 2021)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2010
- **Issuing Authority:** Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ETA2010`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **ss. 6–8:** Legal recognition of electronic records, electronic signatures, and electronic contracts.
  - **Part 2A:** Adopts the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (MLETR), providing full statutory recognition for electronic bills of lading and transferable commercial paper.

### 6. MAS Notice 644 on Technology Risk Management
- **Official Citation:** MAS Notice 644 (issued under Banking Act 1970)
- **Scope:** `SECTORAL` | **Typology:** `REGULATION` | **Year:** 2013 (Amended 2021)
- **Issuing Authority:** Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-644`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Paragraph 4:** Mandatory notification to MAS of any critical IT system malfunction or cyber incident within **1 hour** of incident discovery.
  - Comprehensive controls on multi-factor authentication (MFA), network segregation, encryption, and regular penetration testing.

### 7. Evidence Act 1893 (ss. 35A & 116A - Electronic Records)
- **Official Citation:** Cap. 97 (1893 Act, 2020 Rev Ed, as amended by Evidence (Amendment) Act 2012)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1893
- **Issuing Authority:** Supreme Court of Singapore / Attorney-General's Chambers (AGC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EA1893`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Section 35A:** Admissibility of electronic records and computer outputs: eliminates common law hearsay hurdles for electronic evidence.
  - **Section 116A:** Statutory presumptions regarding the integrity and proper storage of electronic messages and records.

### 8. Online Criminal Harms Act 2023 (OCHA)
- **Official Citation:** Act 24 of 2023
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2023
- **Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/OCHA2023`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 3:** Authorizes the issuance of stop communication directions, disabling directions, and account restriction directions to internet intermediaries to disrupt scam syndicates, phishing websites, and cyber extortion.
  - Failure to comply is an offence punishable by fines up to **SGD 1,000,000** or imprisonment up to **3 years**.

### 9. Goods and Services Tax Act 1993 (Overseas Vendor Registration - OVR)
- **Official Citation:** Act 31 of 1993 (Seventh Schedule)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1993 (Amended 2020/2023)
- **Issuing Authority:** Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS)
- **Direct Link:** `https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/GSTA1993`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Seventh Schedule:** Overseas Vendor Registration (OVR) regime requiring non-resident suppliers of B2C digital services exceeding SGD 100,000 in Singapore supplies to register for and collect GST.
  - Electronic marketplace operators are deemed the supplier responsible for charging and accounting for GST.
