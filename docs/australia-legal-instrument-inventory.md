## Detailed Enactment Dossiers

### 1. Criminal Code Act 1995 (Part 10.7 - Computer Offences)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 12 of 1995 (Schedule 1, Part 10.7, as amended by Cybercrime Act 2001)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `CODE_PROVISION` | **Year:** 1995
- **Issuing Authority:** Commonwealth Attorney-General's Department / Australian Federal Police (AFP)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04868/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 477.1:** Unauthorized access, modification or impairment with intent to commit a serious crime. Imprisonment up to **10 years**.
  - **s. 477.2:** Unauthorized modification of data to cause impairment. Imprisonment up to **10 years**.
  - **s. 477.3:** Unauthorized impairment of electronic communication (DDoS attacks). Imprisonment up to **10 years**.
  - **s. 478.1:** Unauthorized access to, or modification of, restricted data held in a computer. Imprisonment up to **2 years**.
  - **s. 478.3:** Possession or control of data (malware, exploits, botnet code) with intent to commit a computer offence. Imprisonment up to **3 years**.

### 2. Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles & NDB Scheme)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 119 of 1988 (as amended by Privacy Legislation Amendment Act 2022)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1988
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part IIIC:** Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) Scheme: Mandatory notification of eligible data breaches to the Information Commissioner and affected individuals as soon as practicable.
  - **Section 13G:** Penalties for serious or repeated interferences with privacy up to **AUD 50,000,000**, three times the benefit obtained, or 30% of adjusted turnover.

### 3. Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 (SOCI Act)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 29 of 2018 (as amended 2021/2022)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2018
- **Issuing Authority:** Cyber and Infrastructure Security Centre (CISC) / Australian Signals Directorate (ASD)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2018A00029/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 2B:** Mandatory incident reporting: **12-hour notification** for critical cyber security incidents having a significant impact on service availability; **72-hour notification** for other cyber incidents to the Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC).
  - **Part 3A:** Government assistance powers authorizing the Australian Government to gather information, issue directions, or intervene directly to defend critical infrastructure during severe cyber attacks.

### 4. Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979 (Data Retention)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 114 of 1979 (Part 5-1A)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1979
- **Issuing Authority:** Department of Home Affairs / ACMA
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A02124/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 5-1A:** Mandates that telecommunications carriers and carriage service providers retain telecommunications data (subscriber details, source, destination, duration) for a minimum of **2 years**.
  - Prohibits retaining the content or substance of communications without an authorized interception warrant.

### 5. Electronic Transactions Act 1999
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 162 of 1999
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1999
- **Issuing Authority:** Attorney-General's Department
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00553/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 8:** Provides that a transaction is not invalid merely because it took place wholly or partly by means of electronic communications.
  - **ss. 9–11:** Recognizes electronic production of documents, electronic record-keeping, and electronic signatures under Commonwealth law.

### 6. APRA Prudential Standard CPS 234 (Information Security)
- **Official Citation:** CPS 234 (under Banking Act 1959, Insurance Act 1973)
- **Scope:** `SECTORAL` | **Typology:** `REGULATION` | **Year:** 2019
- **Issuing Authority:** Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.apra.gov.au/prudential-standard-cps-234-information-security`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Paragraph 35:** Mandatory notification to APRA within **72 hours** of discovering an information security incident that has had, or has the potential to have, a material impact on the entity or depositors.
  - **Paragraph 36:** Mandatory 24-hour notification if a material information security control vulnerability is not remediated in a timely manner.

### 7. Evidence Act 1995 (Part 2.2 - Electronic Records)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 2 of 1995
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1995
- **Issuing Authority:** Commonwealth Attorney-General / Federal Courts
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04865/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 2.2:** Facilitates the admissibility of electronic business records, emails, and data logs in federal legal proceedings.
  - **ss. 146–147:** Presumptions that devices or processes that produce electronic records are functioning properly unless contrary evidence is established.

### 8. Online Safety Act 2021
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 76 of 2021
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2021
- **Issuing Authority:** eSafety Commissioner
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2021A00076/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 9:** Basic Online Safety Expectations (BOSE) requiring social media services and search engines to take reasonable steps to keep Australian users safe.
  - **Parts 3–5:** 24-hour removal notices for serious cyber-abuse, cyberbullying, and non-consensual sharing of intimate images. Fines up to **AUD 555,000** for non-compliant corporations.

### 9. A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Digital Supplies GST)
- **Official Citation:** Act No. 55 of 1999 (Subdivisions 84-B & 84-C)
- **Scope:** `FEDERAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1999 (Amended 2017)
- **Issuing Authority:** Australian Taxation Office (ATO)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00440/latest/text`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Subdivision 84-B:** Electronic supplies of software, streaming, and digital content by offshore entities to Australian consumers are taxable supplies subject to 10% GST.
  - **Subdivision 84-C:** Electronic distribution platform (EDP) operators (app stores, marketplace platforms) are treated as the supplier liable for collecting and remitting GST.
