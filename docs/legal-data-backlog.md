# CyberLaw Atlas — Legal Research Backlog

This backlog tracks unresearched, partially researched, and pending legal areas across all 48 sovereign jurisdictions. It identifies specific gaps in legislation, regulatory instruments, and sector-specific directives to guide systematic, research-backed expansion without fabricating data.

---

## 1. Prioritization Framework

- **Priority 1 (Critical)**:
  - Major multi-statute jurisdictions where omission of key acts creates a misleading legal picture (e.g. United States privacy sector statutes, EU national NIS2 implementations).
  - High-impact missing categories across top economies (Digital Evidence, Electronic Transactions).
- **Priority 2 (High)**:
  - Critical Infrastructure Protection and Online Fraud legislation across G20/OECD nations.
  - Secondary regulations and executive decrees issued under primary Acts already cataloged.
- **Priority 3 (Medium)**:
  - UNCTAD baseline categories: E-Commerce / E-Transactions and Consumer Protection across developing economies.
  - Periodic legislative amendments and sunset revisions (2024–2026).

## 3. Backlog Maintenance Protocol

1. When a researcher begins work on an item in this backlog, update the status to `In Progress`.
2. Upon completing the 14-step workflow and passing `scripts/validate-legal-data.ts`, update status to `Completed` with the commit hash.
3. If an instrument cannot be verified after exhaustive search of official sources, mark as `Verified - No Specific Legislation (General Law Applies)` with supporting research notes.
