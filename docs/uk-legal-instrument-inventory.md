## Detailed Enactment Dossiers

### 1. Computer Misuse Act 1990
- **Official Citation:** 1990 c. 18 (as amended by Police and Justice Act 2006 & Serious Crime Act 2015)
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1990
- **Issuing Authority:** Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) / Home Office
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/18/contents`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 1:** Unauthorized access to computer material. Imprisonment up to 2 years and statutory fine.
  - **s. 2:** Unauthorized access with intent to commit or facilitate commission of further offences. Imprisonment up to 5 years.
  - **s. 3:** Unauthorized acts with intent to impair, or with recklessness as to impairing, operation of a computer. Imprisonment up to 10 years.
  - **s. 3ZA:** Unauthorized acts causing, or creating risk of, serious damage to human welfare, national security, or the economy. Maximum penalty: **life imprisonment**.
  - **s. 3A:** Making, supplying, or obtaining articles (malware, exploits) for use in computer misuse offences. Imprisonment up to 2 years.

### 2. Data Protection Act 2018 & UK GDPR
- **Official Citation:** 2018 c. 12
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2018
- **Issuing Authority:** Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **UK GDPR Art. 33:** Mandatory personal data breach notification to the Information Commissioner without undue delay and, where feasible, within **72 hours** of becoming aware.
  - **UK GDPR Art. 34:** Direct breach communication to data subjects when high risk to individual rights and freedoms exists.
  - **Part 6 / Art. 83:** Tier 2 administrative penalties up to **£17,500,000** or **4% of total worldwide annual turnover**.

### 3. The Network and Information Systems Regulations 2018 (UK NIS)
- **Official Citation:** SI 2018/506
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `REGULATION` | **Year:** 2018
- **Issuing Authority:** National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) / DESNZ / Ofcom
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/506/contents/made`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Reg. 11:** Obligation on Operators of Essential Services (OES) to take appropriate and proportionate technical and organizational measures to manage risk.
  - **Reg. 12:** Mandatory notification of any network/information system incident having a significant impact to the designated competent authority without undue delay and no later than **72 hours**.
  - **Reg. 18 & Sch. 4:** Enforcement notices and tiered financial penalties up to **£17,000,000**.

### 4. Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021
- **Official Citation:** 2021 c. 31
- **Scope:** `SECTORAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2021
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of Communications (Ofcom)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/31/enacted`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 105A:** Imposes duties on providers of public electronic communications networks and services to take security measures to prevent and mitigate security compromises.
  - **s. 105L:** Mandatory duty to inform Ofcom of any security compromise that has a significant effect on the operation of the network.
  - **s. 105V:** Penalties for non-compliance up to **10% of turnover** or **£100,000 per day** for continuing violations.

### 5. Electronic Communications Act 2000
- **Official Citation:** 2000 c. 7
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2000
- **Issuing Authority:** Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) / ICO
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/7/contents`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 7:** Electronic signatures incorporated into or logically associated with electronic communications are legally admissible in evidence regarding authenticity and integrity.
  - **s. 8:** Grants ministers regulatory powers to remove paper-based legal obstacles and enable electronic transactions across government and private commerce.

### 6. Fraud Act 2006
- **Official Citation:** 2006 c. 35
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2006
- **Issuing Authority:** Serious Fraud Office (SFO) / Crown Prosecution Service (CPS)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/35/contents`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 1 & 2:** Fraud by false representation, statutory offence covering online identity theft, phishing schemes, and wire fraud. Maximum sentence: **10 years imprisonment**.
  - **s. 6 & 7:** Possession, manufacture, or distribution of articles (including cyber extortion tools, phishing templates, and malicious scripts) for use in fraud.

### 7. Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (PACE Computer Powers)
- **Official Citation:** 1984 c. 60, ss. 19–20
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1984
- **Issuing Authority:** Home Office / UK Police Services
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60/section/19`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **s. 19(4):** Statutory power of a constable to require information contained in a computer to be produced in a form in which it can be taken away and in which it is visible and legible.
  - **s. 20:** Explicitly extends statutory powers of search and seizure to electronic and computerized records during criminal investigations.

### 8. Online Safety Act 2023
- **Official Citation:** 2023 c. 50
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 2023
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of Communications (Ofcom)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/contents/enacted`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Part 3, Ch. 2:** Establishes positive safety duties for internet platforms to prevent individuals from encountering fraudulent advertisements, scam bots, and illegal cyber content.
  - **Part 7:** Grants Ofcom enforcement powers including information access notices, business disruption sanctions, and administrative fines up to **£18,000,000** or **10% of global annual turnover**.

### 9. Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Digital Supplies & Marketplace VAT)
- **Official Citation:** 1994 c. 23, s. 47 & Schedule 4A
- **Scope:** `NATIONAL` | **Typology:** `ACT` | **Year:** 1994
- **Issuing Authority:** HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC)
- **Direct Link:** `https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/contents`
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Schedule 4A, para. 16:** Establishes place of supply rules for electronically supplied services (software, streaming, digital downloads) based on customer location.
  - **s. 47(1A):** Imposes joint and several liability on electronic marketplace operators for unpaid VAT on goods or services sold by overseas traders through digital platforms.
