## Priority 1: High-Impact Common Law & Federated Jurisdictions

### 1. United States (`US`) — Federal Sectoral & State Expansion
- **Current Documented Records:** 3 (CFAA, ECPA, CISA).
- **Candidate Enactments for Extraction:**
  1. *Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA, Pub. L. 104-191)* — Security Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C) and Breach Notification Rule.
  2. *Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6801–6809)* — Safeguards Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 314).
  3. *Children's Online Privacy Protection Act of 1998 (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506)*.
  4. *Cyber Incident Reporting for Critical Infrastructure Act of 2022 (CIRCIA, 6 U.S.C. §§ 681–681g)* — Mandatory 72-hour incident reporting and 24-hour ransom payment reporting.
  5. *Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE Rule 902(13) & (14))* — Certified records generated by an electronic process or system.
  6. *California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA, Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.)* — State statutory benchmark.
  7. *New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500)* — Binding financial sector cybersecurity controls.

### 2. United Kingdom (`GB`) — Post-Brexit & Sectoral Expansion
- **Current Documented Records:** 3 (Computer Misuse Act 1990, DPA 2018 / UK GDPR, Online Safety Act 2023).
- **Candidate Enactments for Extraction:**
  1. *Investigatory Powers Act 2016 (c. 25)* — Equipment interference, communications data retention, and bulk interception warrants.
  2. *Network and Information Systems Regulations 2018 (SI 2018/506 - UK NIS)* — Operators of essential services and digital service providers.
  3. *Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (PACE ss. 19 & 20)* — Powers of seizure regarding computer evidence.
  4. *Electronic Communications Act 2000 (c. 7)* — Legal admissibility of electronic signatures.
  5. *FCA Handbook (SYSC 3.1 & 13.9)* — Binding operational resilience and cyber risk rules for financial institutions.

### 3. Australia (`AU`) — Critical Infrastructure & Evidence Expansion
- **Current Documented Records:** 2 (Criminal Code Part 10.7, Privacy Act 1988).
- **Candidate Enactments for Extraction:**
  1. *Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 (SOCI Act, No. 29, 2018 as amended 2021/2022)* — Positive security obligations, government assistance powers, mandatory incident reporting (12h/72h).
  2. *Evidence Act 1995 (Cth, Part 2.2 - Documents)* — Facilitation of electronic evidence proof.
  3. *Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth, Act No. 162, 1999)*.
  4. *Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979 (TIA Act)* — Data retention provisions (Part 5-1A).
  5. *APRA Prudential Standard CPS 234 (Information Security)* — Binding financial sector controls.

### 4. Canada (`CA`) — Critical Cyber Systems & Evidence
- **Current Documented Records:** 2 (Criminal Code ss. 342.1/430(1.1), PIPEDA).
- **Candidate Enactments for Extraction:**
  1. *Bill C-26: Critical Cyber Systems Protection Act (CCSPA)* — Mandatory cybersecurity programs and reporting for federally regulated critical sectors (finance, telecommunications, energy, transport).
  2. *Canada Evidence Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. C-5, ss. 31.1–31.8)* — Electronic documents and authentication standards.
  3. *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA, Part 2 - Electronic Documents)*.
  4. *OSFI Guideline B-13: Technology and Cyber Risk Management* — Prudential standards for federally regulated financial entities.

### 5. Singapore (`SG`) — Infrastructure & Digital Evidence
- **Current Documented Records:** 3 (Computer Misuse Act, PDPA 2012, Cybersecurity Act 2018).
- **Candidate Enactments for Extraction:**
  1. *Cybersecurity (Amendment) Act 2024* — Expanding regulatory oversight to Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) and Foundation Digital Infrastructure.
  2. *Evidence Act 1893 (Cap 97, ss. 35A & 116A)* — Electronic records admissibility and computer output presumptions.
  3. *Electronic Transactions Act 2010 (Cap 88)* — Adopting UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records.
  4. *MAS Notice 644 / Notice 126 on Technology Risk Management* — Mandatory financial sector cyber incident reporting within 1 hour.

## Priority 2: Major Civil Law & European Union Member States

### 1. Germany (`DE`)
- *IT Security Act 2.0 (BSIG / Zweites IT-Sicherheitsgesetz)*: BSI incident reporting, critical components catalog, attack detection systems.
- *Strafprozessordnung (StPO §§ 100a, 100b)*: Online search and telecommunications surveillance (Quellen-TKÜ).
- *Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (BDSG)*: National supplementary data protection provisions.
- *Telecommunications-Telemedia Data Protection Act (TTDSG)*: Cookies and telecommunications secrecy.

### 2. France (`FR`)
- *Loi pour une République numérique (Law No. 2016-1321)*: Data portability, digital death rights, open algorithms.
- *Code de procédure pénale (Arts. 706-102-1 et seq.)*: Remote forensic capture of computer data.
- *Loi n° 2023-171 du 9 mars 2023 (LOPMI)*: Cyber extortion insurance reimbursement conditional upon filing a complaint within 72 hours.
- *Code des postes et des communications électroniques*: Digital trust and electronic transactions.

### 3. China (`CN`)
- *Data Security Law of the PRC (DSL, 2021)*: Core data and important data classification catalogues.
- *Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL, 2021)*: Cross-border transfer mechanisms (Standard Contract, CAC Security Assessment).
- *Provisions on the Management of Algorithmic Recommendations (2022)*: Algorithmic filing and governance.
- *Administrative Provisions on Deep Synthesis in Internet Information Service (2023)*: AI-generated content watermarking.

### 4. Japan (`JP`)
- *Basic Act on Cybersecurity (Act No. 104 of 2014)*: National Information Security Center (NISC) mandate.
- *Act on the Prohibition of Unauthorized Computer Access (Act No. 128 of 1999)*: Standalone criminalization of unauthorized computer access.
- *Code of Criminal Procedure (Arts. 99-2, 218)*: Seizure of electronic storage media and computer records.
- *Act on Electronic Signatures and Certification Business (Act No. 102 of 2000)*.

### 5. Brazil (`BR`)
- *Marco Civil da Internet (Law No. 12.965 of 2014)*: Net neutrality, connection log retention for 1 year, application log retention for 6 months.
- *Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (LGPD, Law No. 13.709 of 2018)*: National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) regulations.
- *Carolina Dieckmann Law (Law No. 12.737 of 2012)*: Criminal Code electronic intrusion amendments (Art. 154-A).
- *Central Bank Resolution BCB No. 85 of 2021*: Cybersecurity policy for financial institutions.
